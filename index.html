Common questions

How much does a website cost?

Every project is different, so I quote after a short chat about what you need. You’ll get a clear price and timeline before any work starts, no surprises.

How long does it take?

Most business websites take 1–3 weeks from first chat to going live. Bigger systems like booking or shops take a little longer, and I’ll give you an exact timeline in your quote.

Do I need to understand tech?

Not at all. I handle everything technical and explain each step in plain language. You focus on your business; I’ll focus on the technology.

Do you build WordPress and WooCommerce websites?

Yes. WordPress is a great choice when you want to update content yourself, and WooCommerce turns it into a full online shop. I design it, build it, and show you how to use it.

Will my business show up on Google?

I build every site with SEO from day one: fast pages, clear structure and the right keywords. And I offer ongoing SEO so customers in Kisumu and across Kenya find you on Google Search and Maps.

Can you connect M-Pesa payments?

Yes. I’ve integrated M-Pesa into booking systems and online shops, so your customers can pay the way they already trust.

What is business intelligence, in plain language?

It means turning the records you already have (sales, stock, customers) into simple dashboards and reports, so you can see what’s selling, what’s slow, and where the money goes.

Can AI really help a small business?

Yes, practical AI, not hype. Think a helper that answers customer questions, search that finds products by photo, or tools that read and summarise documents. I build it into the systems you already use.

Do you work with businesses outside Kisumu?

Yes. I’m based in Kenya and work with clients across the country and beyond. Most projects run smoothly over WhatsApp, calls and shared previews.

What happens after the website goes live?

I don’t disappear. Maintenance & Growth covers updates, speed, security and support, and I can automate reminders, reports and follow-ups so the site keeps working for you.
